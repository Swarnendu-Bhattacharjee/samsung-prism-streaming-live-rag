  First, Latency Bloat: Sequential document searches force users to stare at

  Second, Token Inflation: Dumping giant chunks into the prompt context burns

  questions like 'What is photosynthesis?', traditional RAG blindly triggers heavy 
  vector searches across the proprietary database.

To solve this, our team engineered a Tri-Mode Speculative Streaming RAG 
architecture powered by Groq LPUs. It combines sub-120ms Time To First Token, 
intelligent query routing that bypasses retrieval when not needed, and context 
sharpening that slashes prompt token consumption by 42.4% while delivering 97% 
grounded faithfulness.

Let me show you exactly how it works in real-time."

   it retrieves verified Samsung specs from local documentation, draws competitor 
   intelligence from the General LLM, and provides a completely objective,
